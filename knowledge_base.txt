Device User Manual

1. Resetting Your Device
To reset your device, press and hold the power button for 10 seconds until the screen turns off. Wait for a few seconds, then press the power button again to restart the device.

2. Battery Life
The device offers up to 10 hours of battery life on a full charge under normal usage conditions. Battery performance may vary depending on usage, settings, and environmental conditions.

3. Charging Instructions
Use the original charger provided in the box. Plug the charger into a power outlet and connect it to your device. A full charge typically takes around 2 hours.

4. Water Damage Policy
This device is not waterproof. Water damage is not covered under warranty. Avoid exposing the device to liquids or high humidity.

5. Warranty Information
The device comes with a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Physical damage, water damage, and unauthorized repairs are not covered.

6. Troubleshooting
If your device is not responding, try restarting it. If the problem persists, perform a factory reset or contact customer support.

7. Connectivity
The device supports Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity. To connect to Wi-Fi, go to Settings > Network > Wi-Fi and select your network.

8. Storage
The device comes with 64GB internal storage. You can expand storage using a compatible memory card up to 256GB.

9. Software Updates
Regular software updates improve performance and security. Go to Settings > System > Updates to check for updates.
