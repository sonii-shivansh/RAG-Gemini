Device Setup Guide

To set up your new device, first charge it fully for 2 hours.
Then press and hold the power button for 3 seconds.
Follow the on-screen setup wizard to complete installation.

Resetting Your Device

To perform a soft reset, hold the power button for 10 seconds.
To perform a factory reset, go to Settings > System > Reset.
Warning: Factory reset will erase all your data permanently.

Battery and Charging

Your device supports fast charging up to 65 watts.
Use only the included charging cable for best results.
Battery life is approximately 12 hours under normal usage.

Warranty Information

This device comes with a 1 year limited manufacturer warranty.
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship.
Physical damage and water damage are not covered by warranty.
